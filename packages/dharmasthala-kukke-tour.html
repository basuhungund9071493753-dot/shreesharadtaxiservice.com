Suggested Itinerary

Morning: Mangalore to Dharmasthala

Begin your journey from the agreed pickup point and travel to Dharmasthala. Temple visits depend on opening hours and queues.

Afternoon: Travel to Kukke Subrahmanya

Continue to Kukke Subrahmanya according to your travel plan and available time.

Return Journey

Return to Mangalore or arrange a longer stay if required. The final itinerary can be adjusted before booking.

Important Travel Tips

  • Check temple timings and any required bookings before travelling.
  • Allow extra time for darshan and traffic.
  • Confirm pickup location, return time and passenger count in advance.
  • Ask us about vehicle options for your group.
