Popular Places in Coorg

Madikeri

Explore the hill town, local markets and surrounding viewpoints.

Abbey Falls

Visit a popular waterfall surrounded by lush vegetation. Access can vary seasonally.

Raja's Seat

Enjoy panoramic views of the surrounding hills and valleys.

Talacauvery

Visit the sacred source area associated with the River Cauvery.

Bhagamandala

Explore an important religious destination near Talacauvery.

Coffee Estates

Enjoy the landscapes of Coorg's coffee-growing region.
