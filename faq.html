Common Questions About Our Taxi Services

If you have another question about your journey, contact us directly by phone or WhatsApp.

How can I book a taxi from Mangalore?

You can contact Shree Sharada Taxi Service by phone or WhatsApp. Please provide your pickup location, destination, travel date and preferred vehicle requirement.

Does Shree Sharada Taxi Service provide Mangalore Airport taxi service?

Yes. Airport taxi service is available for customers travelling to or from Mangalore International Airport. Contact us with your flight and pickup or drop requirements.

Can I book a taxi for long-distance travel from Mangalore?

Yes. Private taxi travel can be arranged for long-distance journeys from Mangalore. Discuss your route and vehicle requirement with our team before booking.

Which destinations can I travel to from Mangalore?

Popular destinations include Bangalore, Goa, Udupi, Gokarna, Murudeshwar, Dharmasthala, Kukke Subrahmanya and Coorg. Other destinations can also be discussed according to your travel requirement.

Can I book a taxi for a temple tour?

Yes. Temple tour taxi services can be arranged from Mangalore for destinations such as Dharmasthala and Kukke Subrahmanya, along with other destinations depending on your itinerary.

What vehicles are available?

Vehicle availability depends on your travel requirement, group size, luggage and travel date. Contact us to discuss the suitable vehicle for your journey.

Can I contact the taxi service through WhatsApp?

Yes. You can contact Shree Sharada Taxi Service through WhatsApp to discuss your pickup location, destination, date and vehicle requirement.
