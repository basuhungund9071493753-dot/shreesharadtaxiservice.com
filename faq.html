Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our taxi and tour services.

How can I book a taxi?

You can call Shree Sharada Taxi Service at +91 63621 77207 or contact us through WhatsApp.

Do you provide Mangalore Airport taxi service?

Yes. We provide taxi transfers to and from Mangalore International Airport.

Do you provide outstation taxi services?

Yes. We provide outstation taxi services from Mangalore to destinations across Karnataka and nearby states.

Which vehicles are available?

Our fleet includes Dzire, Etios, Ertiga, Innova, Innova Crysta, Tempo Traveller, Force Urbania and Mini Bus options.

Do you provide tour packages?

Yes. We provide customised taxi-based tour travel for destinations including Karnataka, Goa, Kerala, Ooty and other popular destinations.

Can I request a vehicle for a family trip?

Yes. We have sedan, SUV and larger group vehicles depending on the size of your group.

How early should I book an airport taxi?

Advance booking is recommended, especially for early-morning and peak travel periods.

Can I customise a tour itinerary?

Yes. You can share your preferred destinations, dates and group size and we can discuss a suitable travel arrangement.
