EXPLORE UDUPI

Popular Places to Visit

Add these destinations to your Udupi sightseeing plan, depending on your available time.

Sri Krishna Matha

Visit the renowned Krishna temple, an important spiritual and cultural landmark in Udupi.

Malpe Beach

Enjoy the coastline, sea views and a relaxing visit to one of the region's popular beaches.

St. Mary's Island

Consider a boat trip to this island known for its distinctive geological formations. Boat services depend on weather and operating schedules.

Kapu Beach

Explore the beach and its well-known lighthouse. Check local access and lighthouse visiting hours before your trip.

Manipal

Explore this university town and its nearby cafés, viewpoints and visitor attractions.
